Core conclusion: Fill information during continuous trading cannot be "fetched" from a single data source — it must be calculated and generated by your simulator based on order book state and MBO events. Time and Sales (T&S) provides a record of "what actually traded in the market," not "what price your algo's order would have filled at."

📊 What T&S Provides and What It Does Not

T&S (Level 1 Trades and Quotes) provides records of executed trades: trade price, volume, timestamp. This is "market fact," not "your fill."

It does not contain:

· Where in the order book queue the trade occurred
· Who was ahead in the queue or behind
· Whether your simulated order would have filled at that moment

🎯 The Correct Way to Generate Fills During Continuous Trading

Your simulator needs to actively compute every simulated fill, rather than querying an external data source. The computation is based on:

1. Order Book State (from MBO Replay)
TRTH provides Level 3 Market by Order data, containing Add Order, Modify Order, and Delete Order messages, each with OrderId, price, quantity, and side. You replay these messages to rebuild the order book.

2. Your Algo Order's Queue Position
When your algo order is injected into the book, it queues behind all existing historical orders. queue_ahead = the total existing quantity at that price level at the moment of injection.

3. Historical Trade Events Consume Liquidity
When a historical trade occurs at your price level, it consumes the queue in FIFO order: historical orders at the front are consumed first, then your algo order.

4. Fill Generation Logic
Only after queue_ahead is fully consumed by historical trades does your algo order begin to generate simulated fills.

🔄 The Correct Use of T&S During Continuous Trading

T&S should not be used to "query fills," but rather to validate and calibrate your simulated fill logic.

Calibration workflow:

· If you have real execution records (your historical orders and actual fills), use T&S to verify: does the simulated fill price fall within the range of historical T&S trade prices?
· If the simulated fill price deviates from the market price shown in T&S, your queue model or matching logic has a bias.
· Adjust the cancellation attribution policy (optimistic/conservative/probabilistic) so that simulated fill characteristics match T&S statistical characteristics.

⚠️ Key Distinctions

Question Data Source Can It Answer?
At what price did the market trade? T&S ✅
Will my algo order fill? Simulator (based on MBO + queue model) ✅
If my order existed, where would the fill occur? Cannot be obtained from historical data ❌ Requires synthetic simulation

T&S is the "observed result"; your simulator computes the "hypothetical result." The two cannot substitute for each other.

💡 Practical Recommendations

Continuous trading sessions: Rely entirely on MBO replay + queue position model to generate simulated fills. T&S is used only for post-hoc validation.

Auction sessions: Since there is no MBO, only the IEP/IEV stream can be used. Fill computation becomes a probabilistic estimate (based on IEP price executability and IEV size constraints), and T&S is likewise used only for calibration.

If Refinitiv Tick History's HKEX MBO coverage is unavailable: You can only fall back to Level 2 aggregated depth, in which case fill generation must use probabilistic cancellation attribution models, and precision will drop significantly.
